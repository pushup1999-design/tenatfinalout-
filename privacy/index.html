Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ten@ Digitals.

At Ten@ Digitals, we value your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, enquire about our services, or interact with our digital platforms and products.

By using our website or engaging with our services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy, subject to any consent required by applicable law.

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected, including:

Where applicable and subject to relevant consent requirements, we may use cookies, pixels, tags, analytics tools, and advertising technologies to understand website activity, measure campaign performance, and improve advertising effectiveness.

If you use our AI-powered interview assessment, aptitude training, or personalized learning products, we may process information such as assessment responses, learning progress, user-provided content, and usage records, as necessary for the relevant product.

Additional notices may apply to specific AI products, particularly where sensitive employment, assessment, or educational decisions are involved.

We may use collected information to:

  • Deliver creative, marketing, advertising, and technology services

We will use personal information for specified and legitimate purposes, with appropriate notice and consent or another lawful basis where required.

Our website and advertising services may use cookies and similar technologies to:

We do not sell personal information as a standalone business activity.
